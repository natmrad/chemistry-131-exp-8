Part 3 · Procedures 3-1 through 3-5
Standardizing the NaOH Solution
Use KHP (potassium hydrogen phthalate, a primary standard) to determine the exact concentration of your NaOH solution. Perform two trials.
Why standardize NaOH? NaOH absorbs CO₂ and moisture from air, so its actual concentration differs from what was prepared. Titrating a precisely weighed primary standard (KHP, MW = 204.23 g/mol) gives the true molarity.
Pre-Lab Calculation
Target: neutralize 10 mL of ~0.1 M NaOH
n(NaOH) = 0.100 M × 0.010 L = 0.00100 mol
KHP + NaOH → KNaP + H₂O  (1 : 1 molar ratio)
mass KHP = 0.00100 mol × 204.23 g/mol = 0.204 g
Your actual weighed mass will differ slightly — this is expected and normal.
